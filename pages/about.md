title: About us
---

In 2018, a group of friends came together with one simple idea: to help as many underprivileged students as possible continue their education. Funded by our own contributions and those of our close circle of friends, we pay school and college fees so these students can keep learning and keep dreaming.

Through our partnership with another non-profit organization, we also run free tuition centers for underprivileged kids, giving them the support they need to learn and grow. And beyond the classroom, we back young talent by supporting students who want to pursue their passion for sports.

Our vision is simple: that more students get to stay in school, whatever their circumstances.

## What we do

- **School and college fees**: Supporting students who need it the most
- **Community tuition centres**: Covering teachers’ stipends and learning materials
- **School supplies**: Providing bags, books, and stationery
- **Medical emergencies**: Offering help in rare, critical cases

## How we decide who to help

Most of our students come to us through our friends and wider circle of acquaintances. We verify each student’s need and admission details with the school or college, and wherever possible, we pay the institution directly rather than handing over cash. Every rupee spent is recorded in our books.
