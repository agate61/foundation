Agate Foundation began as a group of friends who kept pooling money whenever a student we knew couldn't pay their fees. In 2026 we registered as a foundation so that this work could be accountable, transparent and able to grow.

## What we do

- **School and college fees** for students who would otherwise drop out
- **Community tuition centres**: helping with a teacher's stipend, rent or materials
- **School supplies**: bags, uniforms, books and stationery
- **Medical emergencies**: help when a student or their family faces a crisis

## How we decide who to help

Most students are referred to us by teachers. We meet the family, verify the need, and wherever possible pay the school, tuition centre or hospital directly rather than giving cash. Every rupee we spend is recorded on this site and in our books.
